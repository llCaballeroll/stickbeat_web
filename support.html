Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Write to soporte.stickbeat@outlook.com. Tell us your phone model, system version and what you were doing. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

How does the magic cutout work? What if it misses?

Stickbeat detects the main subject of your photo and removes the background right on your phone (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android). Nothing is uploaded. It works best with good light and a subject that stands out from the background. If it misses a part, use the lasso to draw around what you want, the eraser to remove leftovers and the restore brush to bring back what was cut. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in on the edges. On Android, the first cutout may need a connection so Google Play services can download the model once.

Why does WhatsApp need at least 3 stickers?

It's a WhatsApp rule for sticker packs from other apps: each pack must have between 3 and 30 stickers. Add at least 3 stickers to your pack before sending it to WhatsApp.

How do I add a pack to WhatsApp?

Make sure WhatsApp is installed. Open your pack in Stickbeat, tap “Add to WhatsApp” and confirm in WhatsApp. The pack will appear in the stickers tab of any chat.

Do sound stickers work in WhatsApp?

A sound sticker is exported as a short, square MP4 video with your sticker and sound. You can send it in WhatsApp, or any other app, as a video or post it to your status. It is not a native WhatsApp sound sticker: WhatsApp doesn't let other apps add those. Some WhatsApp Beta accounts on Android can create a sound sticker from a video in the gallery; that is a WhatsApp feature and WhatsApp decides the result.

How long can a sound be?

Up to 5 seconds on the free plan and up to 10 seconds with Stickbeat Pro. Pick the part you want on the waveform.

What does the free plan include?

5 stickers and 2 sound stickers a day, 3 packs and 3 fonts, with ads. Watch an optional short video to get more for the day, or get Stickbeat Pro to remove ads and limits.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in Stickbeat and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy Pro.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Deleting the app does not cancel it. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period or free trial ends:

  • iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Stickbeat › Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play app › your profile picture › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Stickbeat › Cancel subscription.

The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment, so there is nothing to cancel.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored only on your phone. Go to Settings › Delete all my stickers, or uninstall the app. Packs you added to WhatsApp stay there; remove them from WhatsApp's sticker settings.

How do I change my ad privacy options?

In Stickbeat, go to Settings › Ad privacy options to review your consent (European Union, United Kingdom and Switzerland) or opt out of the sale or sharing of data in US states with privacy laws. On iPhone you can also turn off tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Stickbeat Pro removes ads completely. More in the privacy policy.

The app can't see my photos or microphone.

Stickbeat only asks for permission the first time you use a feature. If you said no, allow it in your phone's Settings › Stickbeat (iPhone) or Settings › Apps › Stickbeat › Permissions (Android).
