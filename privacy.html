Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

In short

  • No account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your photos, audio, stickers and packs are processed and stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, unless you choose to share them.
  • The background cutout runs on your device (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android).
  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads, never inside the editor.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Stickbeat is an independent app developed by Luis Angel Caballero Jardón, based in Metepec, State of Mexico, Mexico, who is responsible for the processing described in this policy. For any privacy matter, write to soporte.stickbeat@outlook.com.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't upload your photos, audio or stickers to any server. We don't have servers with user data.
  • We don't ask for your location or contacts.
  • We don't build profiles or use your photos to train any model.
  • Ads never appear inside the editor.

4. Ads and consent

The free version shows Google AdMob ads: an ad when opening the app, banners on some screens and optional rewarded videos that give you more stickers for the day. Google may use cookies or device identifiers to show and measure ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

European Union, United Kingdom and Switzerland

The first time you open the app you will see a Google consent form (User Messaging Platform) compatible with the IAB TCF framework. You can accept, refuse or choose purposes. If you refuse, ads will not be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy options.

iPhone

Stickbeat asks for permission through App Tracking Transparency before accessing the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you don't allow it, the app works exactly the same and ads are less personalized.

United States

If you live in a state with a privacy law (for example California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for advertising in Settings › Ad privacy options.

How to limit personalized ads

  • Android: Settings › Google › Ads › Delete advertising ID.
  • iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and Apple Advertising.
  • Stickbeat Pro removes all ads.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Stickbeat Pro is offered as a yearly auto-renewable subscription (with a free trial for eligible new users) or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Both are paid through the App Store or Google Play, which process the payment under their own privacy policies. We only receive confirmation that your purchase is valid. We never receive your card number or bank details. You can restore Pro in Settings › Restore purchases.

6. Children

Stickbeat is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child gave us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Your stickers, packs, sounds and settings stay on your phone until you delete them. Use Settings › Delete all my stickers, or uninstall the app, which removes its folder.
  • Stickers you added to WhatsApp or shared with other apps stay there. Delete them from those apps.
  • Ad and purchase data are kept by Google and Apple under their own policies. You can manage them from your Google or Apple account.

8. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, cancellation or opposition (ARCO rights under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties) and withdraw your consent. If you live in the European Union, the United Kingdom or Switzerland you also have the rights of the GDPR (or its local equivalent), and in California the rights of the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

Since we don't keep user data on our own servers, the fastest way is usually to delete the app's data or use Google's and Apple's privacy tools. If you need anything else, write to soporte.stickbeat@outlook.com with the subject “Privacy”. We reply within 20 business days.

9. Security and transfers

Your photos and audio never leave your phone unless you share them. Stickbeat's communication with Apple, Google and AdMob is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). These providers may process data on servers outside Mexico, mainly in the United States, with the safeguards required by law.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app or on the store listing.
