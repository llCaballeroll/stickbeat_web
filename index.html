Sound stickers

Stickers that
talk back.

Pair a sticker with your voice, a song clip or the sound from a video, and send it anywhere.

  1. Pick a sticker. Any one you've made.
  2. Add a sound. Record your voice, choose an audio file or use the sound of a video.
  3. Trim it. Pick the best part on the waveform: up to 5 seconds, or 10 with Pro.

Sound stickers are saved as a short, square MP4 video you can share in any app. They are not native WhatsApp sound stickers. Some WhatsApp Beta accounts on Android can create sound stickers from a gallery video; WhatsApp decides how that looks.
